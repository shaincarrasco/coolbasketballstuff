      Method & limits. Features: 39 order-invariant lineup features (sums / spreads of shooting, creation, size, defence; shooter, handler and big counts; pairwise and interaction terms). Model: minutes-weighted XGBoost (depth-2, 1 500 rounds) predicts lineup net rating; a second booster predicts offensive rating and defence = off − net. Evaluated with GroupKFold by team, so every number in the lab is on rosters the model never saw. Fit = 100 · sigmoid((net − μ) / σ) with μ, σ from the minutes-weighted history (50 = average, 73 = +1σ, 88 = +2σ). Contributions are XGBoost pred_contribs (exact SHAP values for trees). Opponent mode is a heuristic, not learned: adj = -0.6 · (opp rim BLK% − league) · max(0, our non-shooters − 1) -0.08 · (opp STL% − league) · (our TOV% − league) +0.5 · (our avg height − opp avg height), clipped to ±4; "opp" is the opponent's best feasible five, "league" the mean of all eight teams' best fives. Read it as a direction to think in, not a prediction. Rotation is a heuristic too: minutes budgets are season-minute share × 240 capped at 36; starters 30–34 min, bench 14–18; leftover minutes go to the rest of the roster. Limits: synthetic 8-team league with an invented synergy function; 560 lineups; no uncertainty on predictions; synergy is marginal (averaged over every five containing the pair), not causal; fit scores are for demonstration only. CLI: python cli.py recommend --team X --vs Y --rotation.
