Coaching plan

5 drills
  1. One-motion form shooting
    3 x 20 from 1 m (3 ft)
    Load-to-release time +0.23 s No pause at the set point: dip, lift and release in one continuous motion.
  2. L-shape set-point holds
    3 x 10 holds, 3 s each
    Elbow angle at set point +7° Form the elbow into an L (about 90 deg) at the set point before every rep; check in a mirror.
  3. Full-extension form shooting
    3 x 20 from 1 m
    Elbow extension at release -5° Reach the elbow to full extension at release; the arm should look like a straight line to the rim.
  4. Heel-tap balance shots
    3 x 10 from 2 m
    Trunk lean at release +3° Finish with the hips under the shoulders; land where you jumped from.
  5. Freeze the follow-through
    3 x 15, freeze 2 s each
    Follow-through hold -0.10 s Hold the finish (hand in the rim) for a full two seconds before dropping the arm.

Read: Start with One-motion form shooting — it targets the largest gap (load-to-release time). 5 drills ordered major → minor; drills are generic form-shooting work, not professional coaching.

Method & limits. MediaPipe-layout pose landmarks (33 per frame) → 9 body-size-normalised mechanics features per frame (lengths in torso-lengths, shoulder-mid to hip-mid; 1 torso ≈ 50 cm for an adult) → shot events (onset f10, set point f24, peak wrist velocity f31, release f41, follow-through end f51) → similarity = 60% DTW on z-scored sequences + 40% cosine of a contrastive 1-D conv embedding (250 steps, 9.9 s CPU, final loss 1.791). Tolerances per metric come from compare.py and are heuristics, not biomechanical norms. Landmarks came from the synthetic sidecar (no MediaPipe pose model in this environment). All demo clips are procedurally generated: no real footage, no real player data, no real player names. 2-D pose only — depth, ball flight and outcome are not measured.
