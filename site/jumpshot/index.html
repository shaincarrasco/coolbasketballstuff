How to film

30 seconds of setup = a much better read
  • Side view, shooting arm toward the camera if you can.
  • Whole body + ball in frame — feet to fingertips at release, with headroom.
  • Phone at chest height, steady (lean it on something), 3–5 m away.
  • 2–6 seconds: start just before the dip, end after the follow-through.
  • Good light, and 60 fps if your camera has it (Settings → Camera → Record Video).

Method & limits. MediaPipe Pose (tasks-vision 1.2.2) runs in your browser and finds 33 body landmarks per frame; the analysis engine turns them into nine per-frame mechanics, detects onset → set → release → follow-through, scores eight mechanics checks (elbow at set, knee dip, elbow and shoulder at release, release timing vs the jump peak, follow-through, balance) against ranges from published jump-shot kinematics, and separately matches the time-normalised shot to a reference library with dynamic time warping (+ a small learned encoder where available) for the style match. The published ranges come from 3-D lab studies of skilled shooters; here they are applied to 2-D phone video, so treat them as coaching ranges, not laws. Single camera, 2-D only: depth, ball flight and make/miss are not measured, and angles drift if the camera is not side-on. The references are synthetic stand-ins for pro shooters. Drills are generic form-shooting work, not professional coaching.
