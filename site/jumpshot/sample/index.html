Coaching plan

6 drills
  1. Dip-and-go form shots
    3 x 15 from the free-throw line
    Deepest knee bend in the dip +20° Sit into a quarter squat before the lift; the legs start the shot, the arm finishes it.
  2. Jump-then-release timing reps
    3 x 10 from the free-throw line
    Release vs jump peak -0.08 s Let the legs finish the jump: the ball leaves the hand as you reach the top, not early on the way up.
  3. Set-point under the eye
    3 x 15 from 1.5 m
    Shoulder flexion at release +3° Keep the ball in front of the forehead, not behind the head, so the arm finishes up and out toward the rim.
  4. L-shape set-point holds
    3 x 10 holds, 3 s each
    Elbow angle at set point +0° Form the elbow into an L (about 90 deg) at the set point before every rep; check in a mirror.
  5. One-motion form shooting
    3 x 20 from 1 m (3 ft)
    Load-to-release time +0.23 s No pause at the set point: dip, lift and release in one continuous motion.
  6. Heel-tap balance shots
    3 x 10 from 2 m
    Trunk lean at release +3° Finish with the hips under the shoulders; land where you jumped from.

Read: Start with Dip-and-go form shots — it targets the largest gap (deepest knee bend in the dip). 6 drills: mechanics findings first (largest point loss), then style-match gaps; drills are generic form-shooting work, not professional coaching.

Method & limits. MediaPipe-layout pose landmarks (33 per frame) → 9 body-size-normalised mechanics features per frame (lengths in torso-lengths, shoulder-mid to hip-mid; 1 torso ≈ 50 cm for an adult) → shot events (onset f10, set point f24, peak wrist velocity f31, release f40, follow-through end f51) → headline = mechanics score (8 checks against coaching ranges from published jump-shot kinematics; see Mechanics checks) · style match = 60% DTW on z-scored sequences + 40% cosine of a contrastive 1-D conv embedding (250 steps, 2.0 s CPU, final loss 1.793). Tolerances per metric come from compare.py and are heuristics, not biomechanical norms. Landmarks came from the synthetic sidecar (no MediaPipe pose model in this environment). All demo clips are procedurally generated: no real footage, no real player data, no real player names. 2-D pose only — depth, ball flight and outcome are not measured.
