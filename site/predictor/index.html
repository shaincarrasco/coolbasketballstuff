Method & limits. Real NBA games (2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26, 2026-27) from ESPN's unofficial, undocumented public scoreboard API, regular season only (playoffs and NBA Cup final are flagged; the Cup final is kept for Elo but excluded from standings). Game dates are US/Eastern. The 2026-27 test set is 139 games so far — a small sample, so the hero number will move; the full prior-season holdout is the stable read. ESPN can change or withdraw the endpoint without notice. Features are built by streaming one day at a time so nothing from a game day leaks into that day's rows; a re-derivation assertion runs on every train. Train on seasons 2023-24 + 2024-25 + 2025-26, test on 2026-27, calibrator and hyper-parameters chosen on the last 60 days of training. Elo: K=20, +80 home, margin multiplier, 25% carry-over regression. The simulator uses Elo probabilities (not XGBoost) with in-path updates and random tiebreaks, top-6 = direct playoff spot (no play-in); off/def are points per game, not per possession; no players, injuries, travel or market lines. Three learners roughly tie a one-feature Elo regression — that is the honest result, not a bug. Not betting advice. Nothing here is guaranteed.
