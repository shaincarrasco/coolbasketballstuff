Method & limits. 30 fictional teams, 3 seasons × 1230 games simulated with drifting strength, +2.5 home court, rest effects and injuries, tuned so a perfect oracle wins ~68% (real NBA models land at 65–68%). Features are built by streaming one day at a time so nothing from a game day leaks into that day's rows; a re-derivation assertion runs on every train. Train on seasons 2023-24 + 2024-25, test on 2025-26, calibrator and hyper-parameters chosen on the last 60 days of training. Elo: K=20, +80 home, margin multiplier, 25% carry-over regression. The simulator uses Elo probabilities (not XGBoost) with in-path updates and random tiebreaks; off/def are points per game, not per possession; no players, injuries, travel or market lines. Three learners tie a one-feature Elo regression — that is the honest result, not a bug. Not betting advice. Nothing here is guaranteed.
